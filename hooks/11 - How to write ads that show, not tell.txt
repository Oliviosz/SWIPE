--- BEGIN ORIGINAL WRITTEN LESSON ---

You need to show, not tell... Even with words

The Problem: Most copywriters TELL benefits. But telling triggers the logical brain (skepticism)

The Solution: Write visually 

 write the experience, write the behavior, write the new desire.

Write more CONCRETE.

"This will make you feel energized." → Telling

"This will have you running through a wall" --> Showing

"This wallet is high quality" → Telling 

"This wallet is made with titanium" --> Showing

"This will boost your confidence" → Telling 

"You'll forget you were ever nervous to go out" → Showing

"You'll smell way better" → Telling 

"Girls will chase you around" → Showing (Example below)

Let your prospect tell it to themselves, not you.
