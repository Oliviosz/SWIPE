If your ferritin level is less than 50, are you okay? Listen, low ferritin is one of the most commonly overlooked issues that we see in our perimenopause patients. And surprisingly, it's not even due to lack of testing. Here's the issue. You can have completely normal lab results and still have ferritin levels that are way too low for your body to function properly. The standard lab range for ferritin bottoms out around 15, but in reality, anything under 50 is going to cause some real problems, especially if you're in perimenopause. I'm talking chronic fatigue, hair shedding, cold flashes, internal tremors, restless legs, heart palpitations, thyroid dysfunction. The list goes on and on. We know that low ferritin is especially prevalent during perimenopause. And yet so many women have no idea that low ferritin could be making their symptoms even worse because their doctor isn't even acknowledging that there's a problem here.
